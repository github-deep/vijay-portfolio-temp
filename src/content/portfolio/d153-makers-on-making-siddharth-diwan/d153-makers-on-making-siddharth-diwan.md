By virtue of being a student at the film school, often filmmakers visit to share their learnings.

Today Siddharth Diwan was on campus, the acclaimed cinematographer and an alumnus. He won a National Award in 2021 and has plenty of amazing films to his credit – Qala, Trapped, Bulbbul, Bhavesh Joshi, Titli, Queen, etc.

So, here's what I gathered from his class today:

1. **Pounce**

He was a wildlife photographer for two years before entering films. After entering, he took up every role that knocked his way – from gaffer to AC3 to every role in the cinematography department.

When a friend asked him what the right way is to knock on the right doors, he said:

> "I used to hang out a lot with the ADs, roam around in those circles. I had my then GF in Mumbai, so I often ended up there. I bounced ideas, and when Vasan Bala was making his first picture Peddlers, I was their first choice. There is another way I see working – networking, and for the sake of it. I'm an introvert, so I never actively networked or made a showreel. But it is essential. So yeah, network!"

2. **Preparation**

- Camera test
- Lens test
- Actor test
- Color test

3. **Instincts**

> "The more you prepare, the more instincts work on the set."

> "Always get people better than you."

> "A phone is enough for a storyteller."

He resides on Yari Road, near Leaping Windows Café; a place to pitch my story!
